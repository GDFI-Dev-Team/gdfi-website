2013 – 2018 (Multi-Year Initiations)
Expansion and Diversification of Production and Management Systems of Sea Cucumbers
•	Partners/Type: National Government Agency (NGA) & Academic Institutions – Australian Centre for International Agricultural Research (ACIAR), James Cook University, University of the Sunshine Coast (USC) Australia, University of the Philippines Marine Science Institute (UP-MSI), Southeast Asia Fisheries Development Center (SEAFDEC), Mindanao State University (MSU) Naawan, and the Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR-8). 
•	Timeline: Phase 1 started in April 2013 and ended on December 31, 2017 (or extended to July 31, 2018 per the 2019 records). 
•	Brief Write-up: A research project aimed at developing commercial-scale hatchery and grow-out technologies for the sandfish (Holothuria scabra), adapting systems to social, institutional, and bio-physical settings. It established a 5-hectare sea ranch in Maliwaliw Island. 
Building Resilient Coastal Communities: Permanent Shelter & Livelihood Program
•	Partners/Type: Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) – Give2Asia (G2A). 
•	Timeline: Initiated in 2014, with the resettlement village construction completed in March 2017. A subsequent turnaround/oath-taking event took place on March 12, 2017. 
•	Brief Write-up: Provided 48 permanent concrete houses to families living in high-risk areas on Maliwaliw Island, Salcedo. It also funded a Level 3 potable water system and an ongoing wild bee population restoration/bee-keeping livelihood project. 
Eskwela Haiyan (EH) Scholarship Program
•	Partners/Type: NGO – Eskwela Haiyan. 
•	Timeline: Started in 2015 and runs continuously. 
•	Brief Write-up: A scholarship program helping underprivileged children affected by Typhoon Haiyan stay in school. It provides financial allowances of ₱12,000/year for high school students and ₱5,000 to ₱8,000/semester for state college students. 
Coastal Greenbelt Establishment Project
•	Partners/Type: Academic Institution – University of the Philippines Visayas Tacloban College (UPVTC). 
•	Timeline: Ongoing actively throughout 2017 and 2019. 
•	Brief Write-up: Displaced families moving to the G2A village left vacant coastal spaces where around 1,000 beach trees and bamboos were planted to create a greenbelt buffer against future typhoons and storm surges. 
Endowment Fund for Bantay Dagat / Marine Protected Areas (MPAs)
•	Partners/Type: NGO – Juan and Isabel Zapanta Foundation, Inc. (JIZFI). 
•	Timeline: Continuous endowment established prior to/during 2017 through 2019. 
•	Brief Write-up: JIZFI provided a ₱1.0M endowment fund to GDFI. The accrued income is strictly used to pay insurance premiums and cover capacity-building expenses for GDFI-Bantay Dagat and MPA volunteers. 
2016 – 2018
Lawaan Integrated Ecosystems Conservation Project (LIECP)
•	Partners/Type: NGOs & NGA – UNDP-GEF (SGP5), Foundation for the Philippine Environment (FPE), and the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR). 
•	Timeline: Formally started in September 2016 and ran until May 2018. 
•	Brief Write-up: Adopting a ridge-to-reef framework to build community climate resilience and safeguard Lawaan’s biodiversity. Under this project, GDFI also sponsored the field research expenses of BS Biology graduating students from UPVTC in June 2017 and June 2018. 
2017
IIRR-G2A Disaster Risk Reduction Conference Collaboration
•	Partners/Type: NGOs – International Institute of Rural Reconstruction (IIRR) and Give2Asia. 
•	Timeline: March 7 – 10, 2017. 
•	Brief Write-up: GDFI participated in this conference held at IIRR, Cavite, and the Asian Institute of Management (AIM) to share experiences and learn best practices for building disaster-resilient coastal communities across Asian countries. 
PAMS Scientific Presentation Collaboration
•	Partners/Type: Academic/Scientific Association – Philippine Association of Marine Sciences (PAMS). 
•	Timeline: July 13 – 15, 2017. 
•	Brief Write-up: GDFI research staff presented two scientific papers regarding sandfish/sea cucumber movement and sediment alterations during the 14th National PAMS Symposium in Nasugbu, Batangas. 
First National Biodiversity Congress Input
•	Partners/Type: NGA – Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR). 
•	Timeline: May 22 – 24, 2017. 
•	Brief Write-up: GDFI presented biophysical assessment data on Lawaan ecosystems and Philippine Cockatoo sightings on Homonhon Island to urge policymakers to declare critical habitats or expand protected seascapes. 
2018 – 2023
Increasing Technical Skills Supporting Community-Based Sea Cucumber Production
•	Partners/Type: Academic Institution & NGA – University of the Sunshine Coast (USC), ACIAR, UP-MSI, SEAFDEC, MSU Naawan, BFAR-VIII, and the local POs. 
•	Timeline: Phase 2 commenced in August 2018 and is slated to conclude in 2023. 
•	Brief Write-up: Follow-up phase targeted at scaling up technical skills, expanding community-based sea cucumber farming, and improving culture methods inside sea pen nurseries. 
Building Resilient Coastal Communities: Strengthening Gains of Rehabilitation
•	Partners/Type: NGO – Give2Asia (G2A). 
•	Timeline: Commenced in 2018 and extended through 2019. 
•	Brief Write-up: Focus turned toward creating financial resilience for families via supporting women’s economic livelihood groups (e.g., agro-aqua trade fairs, community stores, and livestock production). 
2019
Alliance of Seven Municipalities for Integrated Coastal Zone Management (A7 for ICZM) Reactivation
•	Partners/Type: NGA & LGUs – BFAR via its FishCORAL Project, alongside the LGUs of Lawaan, Balangiga, Giporlos, Quinapondan, Salcedo, Mercedes, and Guiuan. 
•	Timeline: Re-activation began around 2017 and culminated in major ordinance overhauls throughout 2019. 
•	Brief Write-up: GDFI facilitated the review, revision, and ongoing integration of a Uniform Municipal Fisheries Ordinance (MFO) across seven local government units to harmonize marine resource governance across 24 MPAs in the northern Leyte Gulf. 
MPA Networking and Fisherfolk Day Celebration
•	Partners/Type: NGAs – Department of Agriculture - Philippine Rural Development Project (DA-PRDP) and BFAR FishCORAL. 
•	Timeline: Celebrated on September 18, 2019. 
•	Brief Write-up: Coordinated an alliance-wide event hosted by LGU Quinapondan to honor local fishers and judge the "Best Managed MPA" competition. DA-PRDP contributed engine raffle items and funding for the marine sanctuary network infrastructure. 
Non-Government Organization Disaster Preparedness Program (NGODPP)
•	Partners/Type: NGO – Give2Asia. 
•	Timeline: Heavily highlighted in 2019 (tested by typhoons in November and December 24, 2019). 
•	Brief Write-up: Provided solar-powered radio communications systems, sirens, community drills, and a seaworthy passenger/cargo boat launched on August 30, 2019, to improve emergency logistics for the isolated island of Suluan.

Blue Carbon Sediment Assessment (INSPIRE Project)
•	Partners/Type: NGO – Institute for Climate and Sustainable Cities (ICSC).
•	Timeline: Active during 2023.
•	Brief Write-up: Under the "INSPIRE Project for Inclusive Growth and Regenerative Ecosystems" and the "MAP-ELCA" project, GDFI partnered with ICSC to map and assess blue carbon sediment stock (carbon stored in mangroves and seagrasses) in selected areas of Guiuan, Salcedo, Mercedes, Paranas, and Samar to mitigate climate change.

•	Partners/Type: NGO – Environmental Defense Fund (EDF).
•	Timeline: Active during 2023.
•	Brief Write-up: EDF became an official partner of GDFI to support holistic fisheries management planning within the A7 for ICZM areas. Through EDF's Small Scale Fishers (SSF) Hub, the project facilitated cross-border interaction and knowledge sharing between municipal fishers in the A7 communities and international fishers.

Bagonbanua Training and Research Station Fund Campaign
•	Partners/Type: Various Individual Donors.
•	Timeline: Active campaign during 2023.

The station is intended to provide hands-on training in coastal resource assessment and monitoring for students, researchers, and study groups.

2025
Regional & Inter-Local Alliances
•	The Alliance of Seven (A7): GDFI acts as a core partner and technical driver for this alliance, which includes seven coastal municipalities in Eastern Samar sharing the northern Leyte Gulf (Guiuan, Mercedes, Salcedo, Quinapondan, Giporlos, Balangiga, and Lawaan). GDFI facilitated the creation of the A7 Strategic Plan for 2026–2030 to unify regional fisheries management. 
•	A7 Marine Protected Area (MPA) Monitoring Team: Partnered directly with local communities to train 28 representatives to systematically track coral reef, seagrass, and mangrove health. 
International & Local NGOs / Funding Partners
•	Canada Fund for Local Initiatives (CFLI): Partnered on projects aimed at climate change adaptation and governance. This collaboration directly funded the community-led rehabilitation of 18 hectares of mangroves across Maliwaliw Island, Bungtod, and Balangiga. 
•	COAST Facility Philippines: Partnered on Project SAGIP-Eastern Samar. This initiative comprises five workstreams focused on ecosystem restoration, inclusive governance, economic empowerment, and strengthening local fisherfolk associations. 
•	Rare Philippines: Collaborated on capacity-building programs, specifically co-leading the intensive training for the A7 MPA Monitoring Team to improve community-led fisheries management. 
•	Institute for Climate and Sustainable Cities (ICSC): Collaborated on data collection and policy analysis regarding Community-Based Forest Management Areas (CBFMAs) and local coastal ecosystems. 
•	Myriad USA: Partners with GDFI to facilitate international, US tax-deductible donor funding for the foundation's marine conservation and educational sponsorship programs.

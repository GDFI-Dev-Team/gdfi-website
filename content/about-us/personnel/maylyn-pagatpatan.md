bio: >-
  As Project Coordinator, she helps plan, coordinate, and oversee projects that
  support environmental conservation, climate resilience, and community
  development. She works closely with partners, local stakeholders, and
  communities to ensure that  initiatives are implemented effectively and create
  meaningful and lasting impacts for both people and nature. She is passionate
  about protecting our coastal and marine ecosystems while helping communities
  improve their quality of life. She believes that conservation is most
  effective when local people are empowered to participate in managing and
  protecting their natural resources. Seeing communities become more resilient
  and environmentally conscious motivates her to continue this work.  Outside of
  work, Maylyn enjoys traveling, exploring nature, and spending quality time
  with family and friends. A fun fact about her is that she never says no to a
  good bowl of halo-halo or a delicious serving of pansit—two of her all-time
  favorite Filipino comfort foods! She also enjoys discovering new places and
  learning from the people and communities she meets along the way.

## Overview

GDFI mobilized resources from individual donors for a fundraising campaign aimed at rebuilding the Bagonbanua Training and Research Station, originally destroyed by Typhoon Haiyan. The station is intended to provide hands-on training in coastal resource assessment and monitoring for students, researchers, and study groups.

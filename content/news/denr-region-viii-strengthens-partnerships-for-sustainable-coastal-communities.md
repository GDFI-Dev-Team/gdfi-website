The Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) Region VIII reaffirmed its commitment to protecting coastal and marine resources as it joined the signing of a Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) with the Alliance of Seven Municipalities for Integrated Coastal Zone Management (A7 for ICZM) in Guiuan, Eastern Samar.

The alliance is composed of the municipalities of Lawaan, Balangiga, Giporlos, Quinapondan, Salcedo, Mercedes, and Guiuan, together with the Provincial Government of Eastern Samar, Eastern Samar Provincial Police Office (ESPPO), DENR Region 8, Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR) Region 8, Guiuan Development Foundation, Inc. (GDFI), and the University of the Philippines Tacloban College, among other partners.

The MOA, signed alongside the seven municipalities and partners strengthens collective efforts to address pressing coastal and fisheries concerns — including illegal fishing, declining fish catch, and habitat degradation — through harmonized policies, coordinated enforcement, and active community participation.

DENR Regional Executive Director Arturo N. Salazar expressed the agency's full support for the alliance's mission, highlighting that A7 for ICZM serves as a strong model of environmental stewardship and inter-local cooperation. He emphasized that DENR remains committed to working hand-in-hand with local governments in safeguarding Eastern Samar's shared marine ecosystems.

For more than two decades, the A7 for ICZM has stood as a model of collaborative environmental governance in the region. With DENR's continued partnership, the alliance moves forward in embodying the values of unity, shared responsibility, and strong local leadership in the long-term protection and sustainable management of Eastern Samar's coastal and marine environment.

## Overview

GDFI acts as a core partner and technical driver for the Alliance of Seven coastal municipalities sharing the northern Leyte Gulf. GDFI facilitated the creation of the A7 Strategic Plan for 2026–2030 to unify regional fisheries management across the seven LGUs.
